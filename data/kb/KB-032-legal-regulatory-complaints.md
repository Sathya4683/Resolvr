## Symptoms
- Customer says they will send or have sent a legal notice.
- Customer mentions the consumer forum/court, TRAI, the telecom ombudsman or the appellate authority.
- Customer shares a complaint number from a regulator or government grievance portal.

## Likely causes
- Unresolved repeat complaints, billing disputes or service failures.

## Steps
1. Stay calm and professional. Do not argue, threaten or discourage the customer from approaching any authority.
2. Do **not** admit liability, promise compensation or commit to timelines on behalf of the company.
3. Record the exact words used, any notice/complaint reference numbers and the documents mentioned.
4. Summarise the full history of the customer's previous complaints in the ticket.
5. Mark the case as **critical - legal/regulatory**. The proposed resolution must be approved by a supervisor/admin before it is shared with the customer.
6. Tell the customer that a senior team member will contact them within 24 hours.

## Escalate when
- Always. Every legal or regulatory case needs admin approval before any response is shared.
