## Symptoms
- Customer paid for a new connection but the technician has not come.
- Installation appointment was missed or rescheduled many times.

## Likely causes
- Feasibility issue (no free port in the building, society permission pending).
- Technician scheduling backlog in the area.

## Steps
1. Check the order status in CRM: feasibility, permission, scheduled date.
2. Standard installation time is **48-72 hours** after payment when feasibility is clear.
3. If the appointment was missed, apologise and book the earliest slot. Mark the order as priority.
4. If society/building permission is pending, explain what is needed (NOC letter template is in the order page) and share it with the customer.
5. If feasibility fails, offer a full refund of the installation amount within 7 working days, or the 5G home router as an alternative (KB-010).

## Escalate when
- Installation is pending for more than 7 days after payment.
- The customer asks for compensation for the delay (follow KB-034).
