## Symptoms
- Postpaid bill has high roaming charges after a trip.
- Customer says they had an IR pack but were still charged.

## Likely causes
- Usage outside the pack (country not covered, pack expired, usage beyond pack limit).
- Usage in flight or on a ship network (never covered by packs).

## Steps
1. Pull the roaming usage records (date, country, network, MB used).
2. Compare the usage with the pack validity and the covered countries.
3. Explain each charge clearly. Charges for flight/ship networks are not covered by any pack.
4. If the pack was active and the usage was within limits, the charge is a system error: raise a **billing correction** (agents can approve corrections up to Rs 2,000).
5. For goodwill waivers on valid charges, follow the compensation policy (KB-034).

## Escalate when
- Correction above Rs 2,000 or the customer asks for a goodwill waiver on valid charges.
