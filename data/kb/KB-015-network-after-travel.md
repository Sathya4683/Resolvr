## Symptoms
- Phone shows no network or no data after travelling to another state.
- Calls work but mobile data does not.

## Likely causes
- Phone locked on the home network or a manual operator selection.
- Data roaming setting is off (needed for national roaming on some phones).

## Steps
1. Ask the customer to restart the phone.
2. Set network selection to **Automatic**.
3. Turn on **data roaming** in the phone settings (national roaming is free; no extra charges).
4. Reset the APN to default (KB-016).
5. If still not working, refresh the subscriber profile from CRM and ask the customer to restart once more.

## Escalate when
- The profile refresh fails or the issue continues for more than 24 hours.
