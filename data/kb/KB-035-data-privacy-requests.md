## Symptoms
- Customer asks for their personal data to be deleted or wants a copy of what we store.
- Customer asks for call detail records (CDR) of their own or someone else's number.
- Customer says their data was leaked or sold to marketers.

## Likely causes
- Spam/telemarketing calls make customers suspect a data leak.
- Customers going through a legal case need CDRs.

## Steps
1. Verify the customer identity before discussing anything.
2. Never share call records or personal data of **any other** number, even with a family member.
3. Own CDR requests: the customer can download the last 6 months from the MyNet app; older records need a written request.
4. Deletion and data access requests are handled by the privacy team: log the request with the exact wording.
5. For data leak allegations, do not confirm or deny anything. Record the details and mark the case as **critical - privacy** for admin review.
6. For spam calls, help the customer register on DND (send `START 0` to 1909).

## Escalate when
- Data deletion, data leak or third-party data requests - these always need admin approval.
