## Symptoms
- Customer is moving home and wants the same connection at the new address.

## Likely causes
- Not applicable (service request).

## Steps
1. Raise a **shifting request** in CRM with the new address and preferred date.
2. Run the feasibility check for the new address. Share the result with the customer within 24 hours.
3. Explain shifting charges as per the tariff (waived for annual plan customers once a year).
4. The router can be carried by the customer. The technician will set up the new line and re-provision it.
5. Billing continues on the same account. Pause billing for the days without service if the gap is more than 3 days.

## Escalate when
- The new address is not feasible (offer closure without penalty or a 5G home router).
- Shifting is delayed beyond the promised date by more than 3 days.
