## Symptoms
- Customer wants a faster/cheaper plan or more data.
- Customer asks what plans are available.

## Likely causes
- Not applicable (service request).

## Steps
1. Check the current plan, usage pattern and any lock-in period (annual plans).
2. Suggest a suitable plan based on usage (for example, a higher FUP plan if the customer hits the limit every month).
3. **Upgrades** apply immediately; the difference is charged pro-rata.
4. **Downgrades** apply from the next billing cycle.
5. Annual plans can be upgraded anytime; downgrading during lock-in has no penalty only at renewal.
6. Confirm the change by SMS and email.

## Escalate when
- The customer was moved to a plan without consent (raise a billing correction and restore the old plan).
