## Symptoms
- Several customers from the same area report no service at the same time.
- Outage dashboard shows an active incident for the area.

## Likely causes
- Fibre cut on a feeder route (road digging, metro work).
- Power failure at the exchange or tower site.
- Equipment failure on an OLT/tower.

## Steps
1. Check the outage dashboard with the customer's pincode before troubleshooting. Do not ask the customer to reset equipment during a known outage.
2. Tag the ticket to the active incident ID so the customer gets automatic updates.
3. Share the Estimated Time of Restoration (ETR) from the dashboard. Do not promise a time that is not on the dashboard.
4. If there is no incident yet but 4 or more similar complaints come from the same area within an hour, report a **suspected outage** to the NOC.
5. After restoration, the customer gets an SMS. Outages longer than 24 hours get an automatic pro-rata credit on the next bill.

## Escalate when
- ETR has passed and service is not restored.
- Business or critical-service customers (hospitals, emergency services) are affected.
- The customer demands compensation beyond the automatic credit (KB-034).
