## Symptoms
- TV shows "No signal" or an error code like E48-32 on all channels.
- Picture freezes or breaks into blocks.

## Likely causes
- Heavy rain or cloud cover (rain fade, usually temporary).
- Dish moved by wind, birds, or work on the roof.
- Loose or damaged cable between the dish and the set-top box.

## Steps
1. Ask whether it is raining heavily. If yes, explain that signal returns once the rain reduces (usually within 30 minutes).
2. Ask the customer to check that the cable is firmly connected at the back of the set-top box and is not cut or chewed.
3. Restart the set-top box from the main power switch.
4. Open Menu > Settings > Signal strength. If the strength is below 40%, the dish is likely misaligned.
5. Raise a **dish alignment** service request. Visits are free within the service plan.

## Escalate when
- No signal for more than 6 hours in clear weather.
- Many customers in one area report no signal at the same time (outage, KB-031).
