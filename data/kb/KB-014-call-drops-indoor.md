## Symptoms
- Calls drop in the middle, especially at home or office.
- Signal is 1 bar indoors but fine outside.

## Likely causes
- Building materials blocking the signal (basements, concrete, glass).
- Distance from the tower.
- Phone on an old software version.

## Steps
1. Ask the customer where the drops happen and whether they happen outdoors too.
2. If indoors only, enable **WiFi calling (VoWiFi)** on the phone. It is free and uses home broadband for calls.
3. Make sure VoLTE is enabled and the phone software is up to date.
4. Check the coverage map for the address. If the location is marked as poor coverage, log a **coverage feedback** for the planning team.
5. If drops happen outdoors as well, raise a **network complaint** with the exact locations and times.

## Escalate when
- Drops continue outdoors for more than 3 days.
- Several complaints come from the same locality (possible tower issue).
