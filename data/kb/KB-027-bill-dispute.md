## Symptoms
- Bill amount is higher than the plan rental.
- Customer sees charges they do not recognise (value-added services, late fees, one-time charges).

## Likely causes
- Pro-rata charges after a plan change.
- Value-added services (caller tunes, subscriptions) activated.
- Late payment fee from the previous cycle.
- Billing system error.

## Steps
1. Walk the customer through the bill: rental, pro-rata, usage, one-time charges, taxes.
2. Check value-added services in CRM. If the customer did not request one, deactivate it and refund the charge.
3. If the charge is a system error, raise a **billing correction**. Agents can approve corrections up to Rs 2,000; it reflects in the next bill.
4. Keep the disputed amount on hold so no late fee applies while the dispute is open.
5. Send the customer the dispute ID and expected resolution date (7 working days).

## Escalate when
- Correction above Rs 2,000.
- The customer mentions a legal notice or consumer forum (KB-032).
