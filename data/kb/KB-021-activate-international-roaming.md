## Symptoms
- Customer is travelling abroad and wants roaming.
- Phone has no network after landing in another country.

## Likely causes
- International roaming (IR) service not activated before travel.
- Data roaming off in phone settings.

## Steps
1. Activate the IR service from CRM or ask the customer to do it in the MyNet app (prepaid: buy an IR pack; postpaid: enable IR, a security deposit may apply).
2. Recommend buying an IR pack for the destination country, which is much cheaper than pay-per-use rates.
3. After landing, turn on **data roaming** and restart the phone. Select the partner network shown in the app if automatic selection fails.
4. Share the IR helpline number that works free from abroad.

## Escalate when
- IR is active but the phone has no network on any partner operator for more than 2 hours.
