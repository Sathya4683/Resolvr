## Symptoms
- Customer asks for a refund, bill waiver or compensation for downtime, delays or poor service.
- Customer asks for money back for a full month or more.

## Likely causes
- Long outages, repeated faults, missed installation or technician visits.

## Steps
1. Check the service history: outage duration, number of complaints, missed visits.
2. Agents **can** offer without approval:
   - Automatic pro-rata credit for outages longer than 24 hours.
   - A goodwill credit up to **Rs 500** or one week of rental, once in 6 months.
3. Anything above that needs supervisor/admin approval:
   - Refunds or waivers above Rs 500.
   - Full-month rental waivers, cash refunds to bank accounts, device cost refunds.
   - Any compensation linked to a legal/regulatory threat (KB-032).
4. Never promise an amount before approval. Tell the customer the request is under review and give the ticket ID.
5. Mark the case as **critical - compensation** and attach the service history.

## Escalate when
- The requested amount is above the agent limit or the customer asks for compensation in cash.
