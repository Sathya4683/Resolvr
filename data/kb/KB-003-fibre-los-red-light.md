## Symptoms
- The **LOS** light on the router/ONT is red or blinking red.
- PON light is off and there is no internet at all.

## Likely causes
- Fibre cable cut or damaged (construction work, rodents, building repairs).
- Fibre patch cord pulled out of the ONT.
- Fault at the splitter or distribution box in the building.

## Steps
1. Confirm the LOS light status with the customer.
2. Ask the customer to check that the green fibre connector is firmly clicked into the PON port and the cable is not bent or crushed.
3. Check the outage dashboard for the customer's area. If there is a known outage, share the ETR and tag the ticket to the outage.
4. If there is no area outage, raise a **fibre fault** ticket for a field technician. Target visit: within 24 hours (12 hours for business plans).
5. Tell the customer the technician will call before visiting and that no charges apply for fibre repair.

## Escalate when
- The field visit is missed or the fault is not fixed within 48 hours.
- Multiple customers in the same building report LOS together (likely splitter or feeder fault, treat as an outage).
