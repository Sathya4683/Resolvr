## Symptoms
- Customer has auto-pay set up but still got a late fee.
- Auto-debit failed and the service was suspended.

## Likely causes
- Mandate expired or bank card replaced.
- Insufficient balance on the debit date.
- Mandate limit lower than the bill amount.

## Steps
1. Check the mandate status and the failure reason in the payments console.
2. If the failure was on our side (mandate active, balance available), waive the late fee and restore service immediately.
3. If the failure was on the customer side, explain the reason and help them register a new mandate in the MyNet app.
4. One late fee waiver per 12 months is allowed as goodwill for long-term customers.

## Escalate when
- Service suspended for more than 4 hours after payment is received.
