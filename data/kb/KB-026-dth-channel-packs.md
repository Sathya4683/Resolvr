## Symptoms
- Customer wants to add a sports or regional pack, or remove channels to reduce the monthly cost.

## Likely causes
- Not applicable (service request).

## Steps
1. Show the current pack and the monthly amount, including Network Capacity Fee (NCF).
2. Add or remove packs from CRM or guide the customer in the MyNet app. Changes apply within 15 minutes.
3. Explain that removing a pack mid-cycle gives a pro-rata credit to the account balance.
4. Remind the customer that some channels are in the base pack and cannot be removed individually.

## Escalate when
- The customer was charged for a pack they removed (raise a billing correction, KB-027).
