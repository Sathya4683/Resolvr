## Symptoms
- New 5G home router (fixed wireless) shows low signal bars or only 4G.
- Speed changes a lot during the day.
- Router keeps restarting or shows "no SIM".

## Likely causes
- Router placed deep inside the home, away from windows.
- 5G SIM not seated properly or not activated yet.
- Customer location at the edge of 5G coverage.

## Steps
1. Ask the customer to place the router near a window facing the nearest tower, ideally on a higher floor. Use the signal bar indicator in the MyNet app to find the best spot.
2. Check the SIM status in CRM. If activation is pending, complete activation (usually within 4 hours of installation).
3. Power off, re-seat the SIM in the router slot, and power on.
4. Make sure the router firmware is updated (ACS push).
5. If the router shows 4G only, check 5G coverage for the address. If coverage is marginal, offer an outdoor unit installation.

## Escalate when
- Signal stays below 2 bars at the best location (raise a coverage review).
- Router reboots repeatedly after a firmware update (raise device replacement).
