## Symptoms
- Customer wants to switch from physical SIM to eSIM.
- eSIM stopped working after changing the phone.

## Likely causes
- An eSIM profile can only be installed on one phone. A new QR code is needed for a new phone.

## Steps
1. Confirm the phone supports eSIM.
2. Ask the customer to send the eSIM request SMS from the registered number, or request it in the MyNet app.
3. After confirming the OTP and the verification call, the QR code is emailed to the registered email ID.
4. Scan the QR code from Settings > Mobile network > Add eSIM. Activation takes up to 2 hours.
5. The old SIM/eSIM stops working once the new one activates.

## Escalate when
- The customer did not request the eSIM change but got the confirmation SMS (possible fraud, KB-033).
