## Symptoms
- Set-top box has no power light, or is stuck on the logo screen.
- Box restarts again and again.

## Likely causes
- Faulty power adapter or power surge.
- Software update interrupted.

## Steps
1. Check the power socket with another device and check the adapter connection.
2. Unplug the box for 2 minutes and start it again.
3. If stuck on the logo, press and hold the **Power** button on the box for 10 seconds to force a restart. The box may download a software update - ask the customer not to switch it off for 15 minutes.
4. If the box still does not start, raise a **box replacement** request. Free within the 1-year warranty; otherwise as per the repair tariff.

## Escalate when
- The box was replaced in the last 30 days and failed again.
