## Symptoms
- Phone shows "No service" or "Emergency calls only".
- Calls and SMS fail everywhere, not just at home.

## Likely causes
- SIM not seated properly or SIM damaged.
- Number barred for non-payment or KYC pending.
- Manual network selection set to another operator.
- Local tower outage.

## Steps
1. Check in CRM that the number is active (not barred, KYC complete, plan valid).
2. Check the outage dashboard for the customer's area.
3. Ask the customer to switch on airplane mode for 10 seconds and switch it off.
4. Set network selection to **Automatic** (Settings > Mobile network > Network operators).
5. Remove and re-insert the SIM, or try it in another phone.
6. If the SIM does not work in any phone, offer a free SIM replacement at a store (KB-018 for activation).

## Escalate when
- Number is active, no outage is listed and the SIM fails in multiple phones after replacement.
- Many customers in the same area report no signal (treat as outage, KB-031).
