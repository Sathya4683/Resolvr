## Symptoms
- Good speed near the router but weak or no WiFi in bedrooms or upper floors.
- Devices keep switching between networks.

## Likely causes
- Router placed in a corner, inside a cabinet or near the TV/metal objects.
- Thick concrete walls and multiple floors.
- A single router covering a large home.

## Steps
1. Ask where the router is placed. Recommend a central, open and raised location.
2. Ask the customer to keep the router away from microwaves, cordless phones and metal shelves.
3. Explain the difference between 2.4 GHz (longer range, slower) and 5 GHz (faster, shorter range).
4. For homes above 1500 sq ft or with multiple floors, offer a mesh WiFi add-on or extender.
5. If the customer buys their own extender, share the WPS pairing steps or connect it with a LAN cable for best results.

## Escalate when
- The customer wants a technician to reposition the router (chargeable visit as per tariff, unless the original installation was faulty).
