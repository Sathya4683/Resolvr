## Symptoms
- Speed suddenly drops to a fixed low value (for example 2 Mbps or 5 Mbps) late in the billing cycle.
- The customer says the speed was fine earlier in the month.

## Likely causes
- The plan's Fair Usage Policy (FUP) data limit is used up, so speed is reduced until the next cycle.

## Steps
1. Check data usage for the current billing cycle in CRM or the MyNet app.
2. If usage is above the FUP limit, explain the reduced speed and the date it will reset.
3. Offer a data top-up pack or an upgrade to a higher plan (see KB-030 for plan changes).
4. If the customer believes usage is wrong, share the day-wise usage report and suggest checking for unknown devices on the WiFi. Recommend changing the WiFi password (KB-007).
5. If usage is below the limit but the speed profile shows FUP applied, raise a **billing system** ticket to reset the profile.

## Escalate when
- FUP is applied even though usage is below the limit.
- The customer disputes the usage report with evidence.
